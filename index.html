About Society

Restudio Society is the humanitarian initiative of Restudio Software Laboratories, dedicated to making technology and knowledge accessible to everyone, and combating discrimination among developers based on race, origin, or background.

Restudio Society works to help people — especially those facing financial difficulties — access, learn, and understand technology. It does so by providing Restudio products for free and sharing technical knowledge through Restudio Learn, a platform dedicated to documenting Restudio products and explaining how to use them.

Restudio Society believes that financial circumstances, social background, or race should never be a barrier to learning technology or becoming a developer.

Our mission is not just to make technology free, but to make its knowledge, understanding, and access available to as many people as possible.

Our Goals

Combat racial and ethnic discrimination in the developer community.
Provide Restudio products for free to those in need.
Make Restudio product documentation and knowledge available through Restudio Learn.
Help people with limited income learn about technology and programming.
Encourage beginners to enter the development field.
Reduce financial and knowledge barriers to accessing technology.

Restudio Learn

A platform dedicated to documenting Restudio products and explaining their use in a simple way, making technical knowledge accessible to everyone — especially those who cannot afford paid courses or tools.
